From evidence to an audited film

Plain-language walkthrough

The hand-offs from evidence to a film

Read the top row as a relay race where each runner receives a typed folder and returns a typed folder. The key rule is separation: the agents that write are never the agents that check. The checkers see the written plan with fresh instructions, not the writer's reasoning. The director is the only agent allowed to change the plan, and it gets exactly one chance to fix what the auditors flag.

The critical view: what Muse is and is not trusted with

  • Muse never computes ancestry. The percentages come from deterministic math in your browser, or from the numbers you confirmed on a screenshot.
  • Muse never approves its own work. Whoever writes is never the one who checks, and every check is a separate call with a separate instruction.
  • The two auditors are the same model family as the writers. They are separate reviews with separate rules and separate calls, which catches many errors, but it is not the same as an independent human or a different system.
  • If the second attempt still fails, the job ends with a typed error (R4). Nothing is rendered. The gate has no timeout bypass.
  1. 1

    EvidenceRecord in

    Shared contract
    Gets
    The confirmed screenshot shares or the derived raw findings, plus a citation index of reviewed studies.
    Does
    Is made human-readable (lab codes replaced with names) before any agent sees it.
    Hands over
    The evidence and the citation index go to Interpretation.
    Why no model
    Agents receive the same approved facts and nothing else.
    Guard
    Limitations travel with the numbers.
  2. 2

    Interpretation

    Muse Spark 1.3 · high effort
    Gets
    The evidence and the citation index.
    Does
    Chooses four to six citations strictly from the index and writes one reading-level-eight explanation per claim, each tagged with an era.
    Hands over
    Claims (text, explanation, citation id, era) to the profiler and the director.
    Why Muse
    Turning a table of percentages into careful, sourced plain language is writing plus judgement, which is what Spark adds. High effort because it juggles many rules at once.
    Guard
    Cannot change numbers, invent a source or use health, trait or personality stories. Wording must be 'consistent with', never deterministic. An unknown citation id fails the step.
  3. 3

    Population profiler

    Muse Spark 1.3 · high effort
    Gets
    The closest reference populations: panel family, AADR group, locality, country, date, publication.
    Does
    Writes a short card for each: a readable name, who they were, where they lived, why a present-day genome can resemble them, and one caution.
    Hands over
    Profiles, each with a claim id starting 'pop-', to the director.
    Why Muse
    Archive rows read like lab codes. Spark turns them into captions a person can follow with the sound off.
    Guard
    Told to use only the supplied record. No added places, dates, migrations or cultural claims. 'Similar to', never 'descended from'.
  4. 4

    Director

    Muse Spark 1.3 · high effort
    Gets
    The evidence, the profiles, the claims, the list of approved asset ids, and any repair issues from the auditors.
    Does
    Writes the whole timed script: a hook, a method beat, three era chapters, a convergence and a source stamp. Each beat has exact on-screen text, the claim ids it relies on, a qualifier and a bounded picture request.
    Hands over
    One complete ReelPlan of 90 to 150 seconds, validated against a strict schema, to both auditors.
    Why Muse
    Pacing, wording and structure are creative direction under constraints. Code cannot write a documentary, but it can refuse one that breaks the schema.
    Guard
    Can only cite claims it was given. Cannot choose coordinates or run code. Its copy is judged next. High effort gets up to 32,768 tokens because Spark's reasoning counts as output.
  5. 5

    Narrative auditor

    Muse Spark 1.3 · high effort
    Gets
    The plan, the evidence and the claims, in a fresh call with its own instruction.
    Does
    Checks every caption, number, qualifier and cut against the record. Flags a claim with no citation, a missing 'consistent with', a screenshot percentage attached to an ancient group, or a cut that implies a family line.
    Hands over
    A verdict (pass, revise or block) and a list of issues (beat, rule, fix).
    Why Muse
    Judging whether a sentence still means what the evidence supports is language understanding, not string matching.
    Guard
    Runs at the same time as the compliance auditor on the same frozen plan, so neither can influence the other.
  6. 6

    Compliance auditor

    Muse Spark 1.3 · high effort
    Gets
    The complete plan.
    Does
    The final render gate. Blocks health or trait content, personality or identity inference, political framing, conquest or war language, modern nations as heirs, missing citations, and imagery stronger than the evidence.
    Hands over
    Pass, revise or block, with issues.
    Why Muse
    The ancestry-only rule is a policy about meaning. A model can apply it to new wording, where a banned-word list would miss it.
    Guard
    No pass means no film. There is no bypass on timeout or exhausted budget.
  7. 7

    The one repair

    Director again
    Gets
    Both auditors' issues merged into one list.
    Does
    The director rewrites the plan once with those issues in hand, and both auditors review the new plan.
    Hands over
    Either both pass and the plan moves on, or the job stops with R4.
    Why Muse
    A single bounded repair keeps cost predictable and stops the system endlessly rewriting until something slips through.
    Guard
    Exactly one retry. A second failure is final.
  8. 8

    Atlas scenes

    No model, reference table
    Gets
    The approved plan and the evidence.
    Does
    Attaches globe scenes from dated sampling sites in the gazetteer. Places that cannot be resolved are simply left off the map.
    Hands over
    A plan with deterministic map scenes. No new claims.
    Why no model
    Coordinates and dates come from cited records. Memory-based geography from a model could invent a migration route.
    Guard
    Legends say the dots are reference sampling places, similarity not territory or route.
  9. 9

    Illustrate scenes

    Muse Image 1.0 + Muse Spark 1.3 audit
    Gets
    Up to six scene briefs, built from the beats' cited records.
    Does
    Muse Image paints, Spark inspects, and a failed picture gets one targeted edit. Two scenes are worked at a time.
    Hands over
    Reviewed image files with a manifest, or honest fallback plates.
    Why Muse
    Pictures make the history visible. The audit loop is what makes them trustworthy enough to show.
    Guard
    Detailed on the image diagram: one repair per scene, two per episode, reviewed fallback.
  10. 10

    Film ready

    No model
    Gets
    The approved plan, images and cost total.
    Does
    Saves them, emits the final progress event, and tells the page to play.
    Hands over
    The film, the evidence drawer and a cost line for this run.
    Why no model
    The film is assembled by code from approved parts only.
    Guard
    Copy is frozen before this point.
