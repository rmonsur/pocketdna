Two inputs, one EvidenceRecord

Plain-language walkthrough

How two different inputs become the same evidence

Whatever you drop in, the rest of the pipeline sees one object called an EvidenceRecord. A screenshot reaches it through two Muse Spark calls and a human check. A raw file reaches it through math only, with no model at all. This is the first place hand-offs matter: each box passes a typed result, not a conversation.

The critical view: what Muse is and is not trusted with

  • On the screenshot road Muse is only a reader. It copies what the provider printed. It is not asked to interpret, improve or complete the numbers.
  • A person confirms the numbers before anything is paid for downstream, because a misread digit would otherwise be written into a film.
  • On the raw road no model is involved. This is deliberate, and it is what lets the project claim the LLM never computes ancestry.
  1. 1

    Screenshot or raw file

    You
    Gets
    Your file.
    Does
    You choose which road to take: a provider screenshot, or the official raw export.
    Hands over
    A screenshot goes to the gatekeeper. A raw file goes to the browser worker.
    Why you
    You are the only source of your data and the only one who can say whether an extracted number is correct.
    Guard
    Type, size and content checks before anything runs. Unsupported files stop with a typed message.
  2. 2

    Gatekeeper

    Muse Spark 1.3 · low effort
    Gets
    Only the image.
    Does
    Looks at the picture and answers three questions: which company made it, what kind of report it is, and whether to accept it. A health, trait, carrier or wellness panel is rejected (R1). An unreadable image is rejected (R2).
    Hands over
    A small JSON verdict: vendor, report type, route, rejection code, confidence. On rejection the user sees a plain message and the pipeline stops.
    Why Muse
    Telling a health panel from an ancestry panel needs vision and judgement. Keyword rules on pixels cannot do it.
    Guard
    Low effort is enough for a short yes-or-no call, so it is fast and cheap (temperature 0.2, 1,024 token cap). It runs before any expensive step and refuses health data outright.
  3. 3

    Extractor

    Muse Spark 1.3 · medium effort
    Gets
    The same image, with a different instruction.
    Does
    Reads the percentages, ranges and category hierarchy exactly as the provider printed them. Rows it is under half sure about are left out and listed as omitted.
    Hands over
    A list of shares (id, label, weight, confidence) and the omitted rows, to the correction table.
    Why Muse
    Screenshots vary in layout and font. Reading them reliably is exactly what a vision model is good at.
    Guard
    Forbidden to add ancient percentages or personal coordinates. If it sees a health panel it stops with R1. Medium effort (0.4, 2,048 tokens) because digits need care.
  4. 4

    Correction table

    You confirm
    Gets
    The extractor's draft numbers.
    Does
    You edit or confirm every row before generation starts.
    Hands over
    Your corrected vendor and shares become the evidence. The pipeline then skips both vision calls and works from your numbers.
    Why you
    Muse drafts, you approve. This is the one place a human decision sits inside the loop.
    Guard
    Corrected values are recorded as user-confirmed, and the limitations say the findings come from a screenshot only.
  5. 5

    Parse, QC, project and fit

    No model, browser worker
    Gets
    The raw export.
    Does
    Validates and decompresses it, checks marker counts and call rate, projects onto PCA-25, then fits separate Bronze Age, Medieval and Modern mixes with ranges from bootstrapping.
    Hands over
    Derived era results only. Never the genotypes.
    Why no model
    Reproducible population genetics, checked against the Reich Lab's smartpca. A language model adds nothing here except risk.
    Guard
    Low quality or unsupported input is rejected with an explanation. The engine is labelled experimental PCA-25 and is not Global25.
  6. 6

    EvidenceRecord

    Shared contract
    Gets
    Either the confirmed screenshot shares or the derived raw findings.
    Does
    Gives both roads one shape, tagged provider or raw, and carries the limitations along with the numbers.
    Hands over
    This single object is the only thing the Muse agents downstream ever see.
    Why no model
    Every later agent works from the same approved record, so none can quietly bring in numbers of its own.
    Guard
    Only the raw branch carries measured results. A screenshot proves only what the provider reported.
