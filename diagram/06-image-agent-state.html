One scene, from brief to approved picture

A scene is never taken on faith. Muse Image makes a candidate, a different Muse Spark call inspects the actual picture, and only a pass is shown. One targeted repair is allowed, then a reviewed fallback image takes over so the film always plays. This is the image version of the same rule as the script: the generator never grades itself.

The critical view: what Muse is and is not trusted with

  • The pictures are illustration. The prompt calls them reconstructions and the subjects fictional inhabitants of a documented setting, never portraits of the viewer's ancestors and never derived from the viewer's DNA.
  • Muse Image is asked to verify period and regional details with its own search, and the prompt says search supports reconstruction but does not establish provenance.
  • Repairs are bounded: one per scene, two per episode. The re-audit must pass, otherwise the scene is replaced by a labelled fallback.
  1. 1

    Queued

    No model
    Gets
    A scene target from an approved beat.
    Does
    Validates the scene and the mode.
    Hands over
    A valid job to the brief step.
    Why no model
    Bad input should fail before money is spent.
    Guard
    Cancellation prevents any late result entering the film.
  2. 2

    Researching

    No model, cited records
    Gets
    The beat's cited record: place, date, reference family, publication and DOI.
    Does
    Assembles the scene brief from that record and from the reviewed gazetteer.
    Hands over
    A complete brief. A missing required field stops the scene.
    Why no model
    The facts in a picture should come from the record, not a model's recollection.
    Guard
    Present-day labels get an ordinary everyday scene with no costume or ethnic styling inferred.
  3. 3

    Prompt ready

    No model
    Gets
    The brief.
    Does
    Compiles the HIST-IMAGE-01 prompt with every placeholder filled: setting, purpose, action, light, mood, composition and an exclusion list.
    Hands over
    The final prompt.
    Why no model
    A fixed template keeps style, safety and the quiet caption area consistent across scenes.
    Guard
    Excludes battle, weapons as a subject, politics, text, logos, maps and percentages. The left 45 percent of frame stays quiet for captions.
  4. 4

    Generating

    Muse Image 1.0
    Gets
    The prompt, at 1536 by 1024.
    Does
    Paints one cinematic 16:9 candidate. The prompt asks it to check period details with its own search first.
    Hands over
    Picture bytes, hashed, to the audit.
    Why Muse
    It can draw a specific place and century. Costs $0.01 per image and is capped at six scenes, two at a time.
    Guard
    A candidate is not an approved asset.
  5. 5

    Auditing

    Muse Spark 1.3 · medium effort
    Gets
    The actual image and the brief.
    Does
    Compares picture to brief: dates, objects, clothing, landscape, spectacle, text or logos, defects and the caption zone.
    Hands over
    A verdict: pass, repair with an edit instruction, or fallback. Issues are listed by region and severity.
    Why Muse
    Seeing what is wrong in a picture is a vision judgement the generator cannot be trusted to make about itself.
    Guard
    Told not to estimate ancestry or traits from the people shown, and not to certify details too small to inspect.
  6. 6

    Repairing

    Muse Image 1.0 edit
    Gets
    The candidate, attached as a reference, and the auditor's edit instruction.
    Does
    Changes only the flagged error and preserves everything else, including the quiet caption area. If unsure it removes the object.
    Hands over
    A repaired candidate, which goes back to Spark for a second audit.
    Why Muse
    A scoped fix is cheaper and safer than a fresh gamble.
    Guard
    HIST-REPAIR-01, at most one per scene and two per episode. If the re-audit is not a pass, the scene falls back.
  7. 7

    Approved

    No model
    Gets
    A passed image.
    Does
    Writes the file with a manifest: prompt, hash, every attempt and every audit verdict.
    Hands over
    The reviewed asset to the film.
    Why no model
    A provenance trail lets anyone see how a picture was made and checked.
    Guard
    Labelled live, cache or replay in the viewer.
  8. 8

    Fallback

    No model
    Gets
    A failed or exhausted scene.
    Does
    Uses a reviewed plate and says so honestly.
    Hands over
    A usable asset so the film is never blocked.
    Why no model
    A hanging spinner is worse than a plain honest image.
    Guard
    Never silently substituted.
  9. 9

    Cancelled or failed

    No model
    Gets
    A cancel, an uncertain timeout or an exhausted budget.
    Does
    Stops the scene from any state.
    Hands over
    Nothing enters the film late.
    Why no model
    A cancelled job must stay cancelled.
    Guard
    Reachable from every state.
